The directory covers 31 mainland province-level entries. Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan are not supplied by this source and are not fabricated. Municipality placeholder rows are collapsed into the named municipality; province-direct county-level entries use their explicit parent and are offered as destinations without inventing a prefecture. Ordinary counties are not selectable but remain in the private explicit ancestry index. Six-digit identifiers are independent administrative selection identifiers, not a promise of equivalence with every provider version.
